Verdict AI internal analysis, 2026

We live in a time where people argue confidently with facts that are simply wrong. Social media has made it worse. News ecosystems have fractured. And somewhere along the way, the ability to actually hear the other side out became a rare skill.

Most people are not arguing to find the truth. They are arguing to win. Logical fallacies go unchecked because nobody wants to be the one to call it out. Conversations go in circles. Nobody changes their mind.

We built Verdict AI because the best debates are not won by the loudest voice. They are won by the most accurate one. Verdict AI puts truth back at the center of every argument, in real time, with no agenda.

How it works

Four steps.
One verdict.

01

Pick your format

Choose from casual, Public Forum, Lincoln-Douglas, Parliamentary, Policy, or Mock Trial. Each format runs on its actual rules and timing.

02

Set the topic

Pick from a curated preset list or enter your own. Political, social, scientific, or just who was right at dinner.

03

Place and speak

Put the phone between both people. Verdict AI listens through the live mic, transcribes in real time, and flags issues the moment they happen.

04

Get the verdict

Scores update live. When the debate ends, an AI judge delivers a final verdict by points or by argument substance. No appeals.
